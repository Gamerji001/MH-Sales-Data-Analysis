# 📊 Maharashtra Regional Sales Data Analysis & Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project delivers a comprehensive data analysis of regional sales transactions across key cities in Maharashtra (including Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, and Satara). The objective is to clean raw transactional data, analyze revenue streams, and generate actionable business insights using Advanced Excel.

## 📁 Dataset Details
- **File Name:** `MH_SalesData A-2.xlsx`
- **Total Records:** 400+ transactional rows.
- **Key Columns:** Order_ID, Customer, City, Category, Product, Quantity, Unit_Price, Discount_%, Order_Date, Delivery_Date, and Salesperson.

## 🛠️ Key Excel Skills & Techniques Applied
- **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Handled raw data inconsistencies, date formats, and regional formatting.
- **Advanced Formulas & Functions:** Implemented conditional aggregations, lookups, and calculated fields.
- **Data Summarization:** Utilized Pivot Tables and cross-tabulations to evaluate city-wise and category-wise performance.
- **Revenue & Discount Analysis:** Tracked net earnings post-discounts and evaluated top-performing product categories.

## 📈 Key Business Insights
1. **Top Cities:** Major metropolitan hubs like Mumbai and Pune lead in overall sales volume and order frequency.
2. **Category Performance:** High-value categories drive the majority of regional revenues.
3. **Discount Impact:** Analyzed how seasonal discount percentages affect total unit movement and net margins.
